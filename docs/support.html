Support

Email marioszone01@gmail.com with a brief description of the problem and the steps that led to it.

Scan, review, then Dry-run

  1. Run Scan and review the proposed targets, paths and risk descriptions.
  2. Select only items you understand and intend to remove. Keep backups of data you need.
  3. Use Dry-run to inspect the planned work before choosing Clean. Dry-run previews cleanup; it does not perform the planned deletions or guarantee a later result.

Administrator and tool limitations

Some inspections are available with standard permissions. Deep system operations require administrator privileges and preflight checks; available features also depend on Windows configuration, System Restore and installed tools such as WinGet, Docker or WSL. CleanIQ does not automatically elevate itself. If a check blocks an operation, review its explanation before retrying; administrator access alone does not guarantee success.

Partial cleanup and recovery

An operation may skip targets, fail or partially complete. Completed deletions remain, failed targets may be partly removed, and CleanIQ does not automatically roll back the operation. Review the outcome and local history before retrying. A restore point does not guarantee recovery of deleted personal files. Retain and review any WSL backup needed for manual recovery.

What to include in a support request

  • Your CleanIQ version, Windows version and whether you ran with administrator privileges.
  • The feature you used, whether it was a Scan, Dry-run or real operation, and the reported outcome.
  • Reproduction steps and a short, redacted error excerpt or screenshot.

Share logs carefully

Local history is stored at %LOCALAPPDATA%\PC Cleaner Tester\operation_logs. JSON files can contain exact paths, commands, errors and tool output; automatic diagnostic redaction does not cover this history. Review a copy before sharing.

Redact personal paths, usernames, passwords, tokens, private network addresses and unrelated information from logs and screenshots. Send only relevant excerpts. Do not attach full history folders or WSL disk backups. Support emails contain whatever you choose to send, and your email provider handles their transmission.

For storage, retention and external-tool networking details, read the privacy policy.
